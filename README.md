# Football Rebuild Challenge

A football transfer-window rebuild game where you take control of an elite club, make tough sell-or-keep decisions, replace players under budget pressure, and finish with a full season report.

## Short Description

A single-page football rebuild game where you choose Real Madrid, Barcelona, or Bayern Munich, manage a €100M budget, negotiate player sales, sign replacements, complete manager objectives, and see your final squad report.

## Overview

**Football Rebuild Challenge** is a browser-based football management game built in one HTML file using vanilla HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. The game gives you a starting XI, random transfer offers, replacement options, manager cards, financial events, and a final season review.

The goal is simple: rebuild the squad without destroying the budget.

## Features

- Choose between **Real Madrid**, **FC Barcelona**, and **Bayern Munich**
- Interactive football pitch with a full starting XI
- 11-round rebuild format, one decision per squad slot
- Sell, keep, or replace players through transfer offers
- Three replacement tiers: cheap, medium, and expensive
- Dynamic budget tracking for money earned and spent
- Manager objective cards that create extra challenges
- Financial event cards that can help or hurt your rebuild
- Final season report with squad value, spending, earnings, and completed objectives
- Football-card style player visuals with age/photo enrichment and silhouette fallback
- Fully responsive design with a dark stadium-inspired UI

## How to Play

1. Open the game in your browser.
2. Select a club: Real Madrid, Barcelona, or Bayern Munich.
3. Each round, one player receives a transfer offer.
4. Choose whether to sell the player or keep him.
5. If you sell, pick one of the replacement options.
6. Manage your budget carefully across all 11 rounds.
7. Complete manager cards when possible.
8. View your final season report at the end.
